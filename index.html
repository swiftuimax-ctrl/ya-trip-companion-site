Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data. We have no servers and no accounts.
  • Everything you add stays on your device.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Deleting the app, or tapping Erase all my data in Settings, removes it all.

This policy explains how Yaamava Casino Trip Companion ("the app") handles information. The app was built to work without collecting anything about you.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and does not ask for your email, phone number or password. The developer receives no data from the app.

Information stored on your device

Whatever you choose to enter stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Favorite resorts and the interests you pick during setup
  • Trips you plan, with their names, dates, stops and notes
  • Journal entries: ratings, tags, notes, and the optional amounts you spent and your monthly budget
  • Cards and codes you scan or type in, including any name, phone, email or note you add to your own QR code
  • Break-timer and spending-limit settings

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. It is protected by iOS file encryption and is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings.

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app). Used only to show how far each resort is and how long the drive takes. Your location is never stored by the app. To calculate drive times, the starting point is sent to Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Camera. Used only to read the barcode or QR code on a card or ticket you choose to scan. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Calendar (add only). Used only when you tap Add to Calendar to add a trip. The app cannot read your existing events.
  • Notifications. Used only for the break-timer reminder you start yourself. Notifications are scheduled on your device; no push server is involved.

Each permission is optional. You can change it at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Sharing

The app only shares something when you tap Share or Copy. The text or image goes through the standard iOS share sheet or clipboard to the destination you pick. Calls, directions and website links open the resort's own phone line, Apple Maps or the resort's website, which follow their own privacy policies.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

The app does not track you across apps or websites, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is intended for adults planning resort travel. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and tap Erase all my data, or delete the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.

Independence

Yaamava Casino Trip Companion is an independent travel guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of the resorts listed, their owners or any tribal nation. Resort names are used only to identify the places described. The app does not offer gambling of any kind.

Support

Help & questions

Answers to the most common questions. If yours isn't here, write to us.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start planning. There is no sign-in, and everything you add stays on your device.

Why doesn't drive time show up?

Drive time needs location access and an internet connection. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Yaamava Casino Trip Companion and choose While Using the App. Everything else works offline.

A card won't scan. What can I do?

Hold the code flat, fill most of the frame and turn on the torch in a dark room. If it still won't read, add the card by typing its number. The app redraws the barcode from the number.

How do I add a trip to my calendar?

Open the trip in Plan and tap Add to Calendar. The first time, iOS asks for permission to add events. The app can only add, never read, your calendar.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. The app has no cloud account of its own.

How do I delete everything?

Settings in the app → Erase all my data. This can't be undone.

Can I gamble or book rooms in the app?

No. The app is a planner and journal only. Calls and links go straight to each resort's own phone line and website.
